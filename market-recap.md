# Market Recap

Twice-daily market/news recap, written by two scheduled cloud routines: "Pre-Market Recap" (weekdays ~8:55am ET, before the 9:30am open) and "After-Close Recap" (weekdays ~4:10pm ET, after the 4pm close).

Format per entry:

```
## YYYY-MM-DD — Pre-Market / After-Close
- Major indices: S&P 500 / Nasdaq / Dow — level and % change (pre-market futures or the day's close)
- Top news/catalysts: 2-4 bullets on what's moving markets today
- Sectors in focus: what's leading/lagging and why
- Notes: anything relevant to today's/tomorrow's watchlist candidates
```
